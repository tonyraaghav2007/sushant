# Nishant & Shweta Royal Wedding Invitation Website 🌸

An interactive, full-screen scrollytelling luxury royal wedding invitation web experience built for **Nishant & Shweta's Wedding** on **Saturday, 12 December 2026** at **Bhanushali Wadi Banquet Hall, Nerul, Navi Mumbai**.

## ✨ Design & Interactive Highlights

### 1. Full-Screen Royal Scrollytelling Experience
- **Fixed Royal Architectural Stage**:
  - The page backdrop, ornate golden Toran arches, corner filigree borders, floating rose/marigold petals, and atmospheric velvet lighting stay fixed and seamless on screen ("the page stays the same").
  - Content smoothly moves, transitions, and transforms as the user naturally scrolls top-to-bottom with mousewheel, touchpad, touch gestures, or scrollbar.

### 2. Bespoke Transitions Everytime
Every scene features its own tailored, cinematic animation:
1. **Scene 0: Royal Shubh Vivah Cover**
   - Spiritual invocation (*॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥*), Royal Monogram, Couple Typography, and Udaipur Lake Palace sketch.
   - **Transition**: **Gold Dust Scatter & Coalesce** — When leaving Scene 0, words and motifs dissolve into hundreds of sparkling golden stardust particles that blow away into the wind ("dusts away"), and when returning, gold dust converges back into place ("comes back")!
2. **Scene 1: A New Chapter Begins**
   - Palace walk couple portrait with gold foil framing and welcome prose.
   - **Transition**: **3D Depth Zoom & Soft Bokeh Lens Blur** — Emerges from deep space with radiant lighting sweep.
3. **Scene 2: Save The Date & Live Real-Time Countdown**
   - Saturday, 12 December 2026 with live ticking countdown (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds) and 1-click **Add to Google Calendar** and **Apple/iCal (.ics)** export.
   - **Transition**: **Royal Seal Stamp & Golden Shockwave Pulse** — The date strikes into place with an expanding radial gold shockwave aura ring.
4. **Scene 3: The Grand Venue**
   - Bhanushali Wadi Banquet Hall Nerul East address, illuminated mandap visual, Google Maps directions, and 1-click address copy with royal toast.
   - **Transition**: **Symmetrical Architectural Arch Reveal** — Grand palace gates slide open from the center.
5. **Scene 4: Our Story**
   - Heartwarming friendship-to-forever narrative and stacked polaroid keepsake with handwritten "Better Together" script.
   - **Transition**: **Vintage Polaroid Drift & Floating Bokeh Glow** — Soft romantic pendulum tilt drift.
6. **Scene 5: Wedding Events Timeline**
   - Traditional auspicious itinerary (12:00 PM Welcome, 12:30 PM Varmala, 1:30 PM Lunch, 4:00 PM Sacred Pheras, 7:00 PM Dinner).
   - **Transition**: **Sequential Golden Bead Cascade & Alternating Stagger** — Golden thread illuminates from top to bottom with staggered left/right slide.
7. **Scene 6: Your Presence is Our Greatest Gift**
   - Royal maroon & gold silk banner with sacred Shagun motif.
   - **Transition**: **Lotus Blossom Radial Unfurl & Silk Veil Slide** — Central sacred lotus blooms outward.
8. **Scene 7: Grand Finale & Light a Diya**
   - Illuminated twilight Lake Palace photo, couple signature, WhatsApp 1-click sharing, and interactive **"Light & Float a Diya on the Lake 🪔"** button that releases glowing illuminated diyas with water reflections.
   - **Transition**: **Starry Twilight Glow & Water Ripple Wave**.

### 3. Atmospheric Audio & Effects
- **Procedural Indian Wedding Music (Web Audio API)**: Tanpura drone + Bansuri/Sitar raga melody synthesized natively in the browser without external audio dependencies.
- **Floating Petal Shower**: Realistic HTML5 Canvas rose and marigold petals drifting down with flutter physics.
- **Gold Dust Engine**: Real-time canvas particle system for dissolution transitions.
- **Dynamic Specular Light Glint**: Follows cursor / gyroscope with warm golden reflections.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: One-Click Launcher (Windows)
Double-click `launch.bat` inside the folder. It will start the local server and automatically open the invitation in your default web browser.

### Option 2: Live Server
The server is currently running at:
**[http://localhost:8080/](http://localhost:8080/)**

Or open `index.html` directly in any modern browser!
