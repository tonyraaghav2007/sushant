# Nishant & Shweta Royal Wedding Invitation Website 🌸

An interactive, luxury royal card-opening wedding invitation web experience built for **Nishant & Shweta's Wedding** on **Saturday, 12 December 2026** at **Bhanushali Wadi Banquet Hall, Nerul, Navi Mumbai**.

## ✨ Design & Interactive Highlights

### 1. Royal Scalloped Arch Sealed Card
- Authentically styled to match the physical luxury card design:
  - Scalloped Indian/Mughal arch silhouette with gold foil embossed framing.
  - Lord Ganesha gold foil emblem and sacred shloka: *॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥*.
  - Handcrafted golden wax seal with **"N | S"** monogram.
  - Vintage architectural pen-and-ink engraving sketch of the Lake Palace.

### 2. Card Unfolding Scroll Experience
- Tap the golden wax seal or scroll down to break the seal with a golden particle burst and sound effect.
- The 3D envelope flap lifts open (`rotateX`), and the card elevates and progressively unfolds panel by panel as the user scrolls.

### 3. All 12 Sacred Chapters (From Wedding Itinerary Details):
1. **The Royal Invitation Cover**: Names, dates, venue summary, and wax seal.
2. **A New Chapter Begins**: Couple palace walk portrait & heartfelt welcome message.
3. **Save The Date & Live Countdown**: Real-time days, hours, minutes, seconds countdown to 12 Dec 2026, with 1-click **Add to Google Calendar** and **Apple/Outlook iCal (.ics)** download.
4. **The Venue & Directions**: Bhanushali Wadi Banquet Hall Nerul East, floral mandap visual, Google Maps direct link, and 1-click address copy.
5. **Our Story**: Romantic stacked polaroids with "Better Together" script and love timeline milestones from 2020 to 2026.
6. **Wedding Events Schedule**: Golden timeline featuring:
   - 12:00 PM: Guest Arrival and Welcome
   - 12:30 PM: Varmala Ceremony
   - 1:30 PM: Lunch Feast
   - 4:00 PM: Other Rituals & Family Moments
   - 7:00 PM: Dinner & Celebration
7. **Dress Code & Interactive Color Palette**: Traditional Indian ethnic wear guide (Kurta/Sherwani for Men, Saree/Lehenga for Women) with clickable color chips that reveal styling advice.
8. **Pre-Wedding Gallery**: Grid album with fullscreen lightbox and zoom.
9. **Your Presence is Our Greatest Gift**: Royal maroon silk panel with golden shagun envelope motif.
10. **Interactive RSVP**: Guest name, phone, guest counter stepper (+/-), ceremony selection, dietary preference, and submit with celebratory confetti explosion! Generates an authentic **Royal Digital Guest Pass** with pass ID and print/save feature.
11. **Leave Your Blessings (Digital Wishing Well)**: Interactive guestbook where guests pick a blessing flower (Lotus, Rose, Marigold, Jasmine), submit wishes, and like wishes in real-time.
12. **Thank You & Celebration Lake**: Twilight palace with floating golden diyas, interactive **"Light & Float a Diya on the Lake"** button, and 1-click WhatsApp invitation sharing.

### 4. Atmospheric Enhancements
- **Procedural Indian Wedding Music (Web Audio API)**: Tanpura drone + Bansuri/Sitar raga melody synthesized natively in browser (no external MP3 loading failures).
- **Floating Petal Shower**: Realistic rose and marigold petals drifting down the screen with toggle button.
- **Golden Fairy Dust Cursor Trail**: Interactive sparkles trailing behind mouse movement.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: One-Click Launcher (Windows)
Double-click `launch.bat` inside the folder. It will start the local server and automatically open the invitation in your default web browser.

### Option 2: Live Server
The server is currently running at:
**[http://localhost:8080/](http://localhost:8080/)**

Or open `index.html` directly in any modern browser!
